3 · The highway map (HNSW)

    
Each dot is a reel on the meaning map. Click anywhere to place your question.
      Brute force measures the distance to every reel. The highway map starts at one fixed point, follows a few long "highway" links,
      then takes short "street" links, always moving to whichever neighbour is closer to the question. It stops when no neighbour is closer.
